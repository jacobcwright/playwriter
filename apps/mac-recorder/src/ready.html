Playwriter Recorder is ready

Leave this tab open. Record on any other tab in this window.

  1. Open the page where the workflow starts in a new tab, then click the Playwriter icon in the toolbar (top right, next to the address bar) so it turns green. A small Playwriter bar appears on the page.
  2. Turn the microphone on. On the bar, click Mic off so it reads Mic on. The first time, a tab opens with one button, Allow microphone: click it and accept the prompts from Chrome and from macOS. This is remembered.
  3. Click Record Skill and talk. Do the workflow and say what you are doing and why, like explaining it to a colleague. Mistakes are fine: say "ignore that" and keep going.
  4. Click Stop recording. The microphone turns off and a message for your AI agent is copied to the clipboard. Paste it into the agent's chat.

Where recordings go

Everything stays on this Mac, in your home folder under .playwriter/recordings. Each recording has a timeline (<id>.json), screenshots and the microphone audio (narration.webm).

The folder is hidden in Finder: press Cmd + Shift + G and paste ~/.playwriter/recordings.

Good to know

This browser is separate from your everyday Chrome. Sign in to the sites you need once; logins are kept between runs.

If the bar says Relay not connected, quit this browser and open Playwriter Recorder again.
