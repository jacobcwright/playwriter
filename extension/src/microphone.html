Allow your microphone

Playwriter can record your voice while you show a workflow in your browser, so what you say out loud is saved next to each click. Say what you are doing and why, like you would explain it to a colleague.

The audio stays on this computer. It is only recorded while a skill recording is running, and the microphone is released the moment you click Stop.

You only need to do this once. Chrome will show a small prompt at the top of this tab.
